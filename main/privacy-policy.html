Privacy

Your data is safe with us

We collect only what is needed to process your order and keep you updated. Your privacy is respected and protected at every step.

Data we use

We use your name, email, address, and phone number to fulfill orders, schedule delivery, and send order updates.

How we protect it

Your information is stored securely and only accessible to support staff who handle order processing and customer service.

Your rights

You can request access, updates, or deletion of your personal information anytime by contacting us.

No third-party sales

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information when required to complete orders or comply with the law.

Questions?

If you want more details about our privacy practices, reach out through the Contact page. We are happy to explain how we handle your personal data.
