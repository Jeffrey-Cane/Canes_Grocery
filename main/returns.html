Returns

Easy returns for damaged or incorrect items

If your order is not right, we’re here to help. This page explains how to request a return, what qualifies for a refund, and what to expect during the process.

1. Report within 24 hours

Send us a message via the Contact page or email with your order number and a photo if available.

2. Choose replacement or refund

We will offer a replacement for damaged goods or a refund when an item is missing or incorrect.

3. Refund timeline

Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after we confirm the return request.

What’s not eligible

  • Perishable items that have been opened or damaged after delivery.
  • Products returned without notification beyond the 24-hour window.
  • Orders modified by the customer after delivery, such as partial consumption.
