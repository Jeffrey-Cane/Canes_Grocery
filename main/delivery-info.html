Delivery

Delivery made simple, reliable, and local.

From same-day availability to clear fee rules, here is everything you need to know about our delivery service in Nairobi.

Same-day delivery

Orders placed before 2PM are typically delivered on the same day. After 2PM, we schedule the next available delivery slot.

Delivery areas

We serve Nairobi and surrounding neighborhoods. If you are unsure whether your address is covered, contact us and we will confirm it quickly.

Fees & free delivery

Delivery is free for orders over KSh 2,000. Smaller orders may incur a small local delivery fee shown at checkout.

Order updates

We keep you informed with delivery notifications, so you know when your groceries are on the way.

Need help planning delivery?

If you have special delivery instructions or need a different time window, add notes at checkout or contact our support team.

Tip: Save time by pre-entering your address in your account before checkout.
